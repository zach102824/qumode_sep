This is not the collective p-spin and not a random-sign glass.
F is the squared overlap with the full-space ground state.
eta = (Emax - E) / (Emax - E0) of that same full Hamiltonian.
Half-cut entropy is the low n/2 bits versus the high bits, in bits.
