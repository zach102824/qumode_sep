per E.

clause gadget as a Fock-local update: for a span-1 clause the support is an 8-level SNAP phase
mask; for span-2 a joint 8 × 8 SNAP; in all cases the per-clause work is O(poly(n)) (constant in

What this does not claim

• It does not claim a Uc algebraically inequivalent to e−iγHc . Non-Hc probes (surrogate, noncommuting XZ, wrong-graph, adaptive feedback, nonlinear clause phases) all die on n15 screens
versus trunc_Hc / Arm C.
• It does not remove instance dependence: clauses still enter via {Cc }.
• Simulator wall-clock at small n may favor NumPy exp(−iγE) over a pure-Python clause loop;
that is an implementation artifact, not a complexity rebuttal (see docs/COMPILE_OFFLINE.md).
