# Round 3 family N

Full-space N=12 gap is 1.613234e-03. The accessible P=R=+1 gap is 4.337981e-01. They are not the same number.

Family N is Hc plus normalized full K, block-mean M, and the contiguous Neel selector N. One gamma and the three thetas are shared by every layer. This is not a special case of round-2 S: S used inter-only K and a weight on all of Hintra.

The shift that puts one intra spacing back by 2π moves the other spacing by a non-integer number of turns, and the prepared state changes. It is not an exact alias of the intra generator.

Equal depth, both best energy and fidelity versus standard QAOA: 6 of 6 depths. That comparison gives N 3L+4 parameters against QAOA's 2L, and the selector is a level-resolved phase, not a shell SNAP.

Parameter-matched QAOA, depth ceil((3L+4)/2): ahead at no depth.

Versus QAOA at L+1, both metrics hold at L1, L3, L4, L5, L6.

A one-gap 2π shift of the old intra angle is not an exact alias. The ratio of the two intra spacings is not an integer, and the shifted circuit state is different. Raw consultant confidence is not a proof.

The all-start IQR includes failed seeds and is often several energy units. The best-half IQR is also stored. A gap smaller than that spread is not treated as material. No seal is claimed.
