# SATLike convergence study (wmaxsat_conv)

Instances: new unique-GS random weighted 4-SAT (m=round(9.93n), w in 1..5), same generator as wmaxsat_fresh, seeds 5e9+1e7n+k (disjoint). SATLike = strong_classical.runone, ONE cfg for all n: {mode:0, swt:20, sp:0.01, t_bms:3} (chosen by best avg B90 over 4 uniform candidates on held-out 200 inst x2 at n=12,16,20, satlike_uniform_tune.py), cap min(40*2^n,4e6), 5 runs/instance. Strict lookups to first GS check. Unsolved = inf. Std = bootstrap over instances (1000 resamples, instance keeps its runs).
