- **Defaults (λ1=λ3=0):** A0 matches bakeoff exactly (success 0.940, mean|β| 1.844) — Gibbs-only path unchanged.
- **Soft |β| cap with λ1=0** is the winning family for success≥0.90.

- Also ≥15%: λ1=0, λ3=2, β_max=2.1, steps=800 → success 0.900, cut 15.8%.
- Longer SPSA (800–1200) is important for soft-cap to reduce |β| without killing success.

## What failed

- **Pure L1 cannot hit ≥15% cut at success≥0.90.** Mild λ1 (0.05–0.07) cuts only ~5–8%; λ1=0.2 cuts ~19% but success≈0.77.
- **≥20% mean|β| cut at success≥0.90 was not achieved** across 250+ fleets. Settings that reach ≥20% cut land at success≈0.85–0.89.
- **Aggressive λ3 + tight β_max** (e.g. λ3=5, β_max=2.0) can destabilize SPSA (β explosion).
- Adding mild λ1 on top of strong soft-caps usually **hurts success** more than it helps cut at the ≥0.90 frontier.
