# Power-law p=3 TFIM α/Γ sweep

## Hamiltonian

Signed power-law on a **ring**:

$$H = -\sum_{i<j<k} J_{ijk} Z_i Z_j Z_k - \Gamma\sum_i X_i,\quadJ_{ijk}=\xi_{ijk}\, r_{ijk}^{-\alpha}/N$$

- $r_{ijk}$ = ring diameter of $\{i,j,k\}$ (shortest covering arc length in edges)
- $N = (\sum r^{-\alpha})/n$ (Kac-style; classical ferro energy $= -n$)
- $\xi_{ijk}=\pm 1$ Rademacher (seeded) so $N_{\mathrm{inst}}$ instances exist
- **glass baseline:** $J\sim\mathcal N(0,1)$ with scale $\sqrt{3}/n$ (existing `make_instance`)
