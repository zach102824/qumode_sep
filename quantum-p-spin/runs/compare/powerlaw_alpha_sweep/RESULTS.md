# Power-law p=3 TFIM α/Γ sweep — free QAOA

## Hamiltonian

Signed power-law on a **ring** (geometry locked):

$$H = -\sum_{i<j<k} J_{ijk} Z_i Z_j Z_k - \Gamma\sum_i X_i,\quad J_{ijk}=\xi_{ijk}\, r_{ijk}^{-\alpha}/N$$

- $r_{ijk}$ = ring diameter of $\{i,j,k\}$ (shortest covering arc, edge length; min 2 for distinct sites)
- $N = (\sum_t r_t^{-\alpha})/n$ (Kac-style; ferro classical all-$+$ energy $= -n$)
- $\xi_{ijk}=\pm 1$ Rademacher per instance (seeded) — enables $N=16$ instance medians
- **glass baseline:** $J\sim\mathcal N(0,1)$ with scale $\sqrt{3}/n$ (`make_instance`)
